A scan gave the wrong result

Tell us from inside the app, because the report then carries what we need to find the problem. On the result screen, tap Feedback at the top left. From the scanner, use the Feedback icon at the top left, or Feedback in Settings (the gear at the top right).

The app opens a draft email in your own mail app with the photo of the label, the text the app read and the result it reached. Nothing is sent until you press send yourself. The privacy policy describes exactly what a report contains.

What a report contains

The camera does not start

The app needs the camera to read the label. If you declined access, the scanner shows a button that opens your phone's settings. You can also go there yourself: on iPhone, Settings → Sweetener Guide → Camera; on Android, Settings → Apps → Sweetener Guide → Permissions.

Getting a good scan

Hold the phone steady over the ingredients list, in good light, with the text inside the frame. Tap the screen to focus on the text if it looks blurred. Curved or shiny packaging reads better when you tilt it a little to avoid reflections.

Free scans and the subscription

The first ten scans are free. After that, scanning needs a subscription, monthly or yearly. The price is shown in the app before you confirm. A subscription belongs to your App Store or Google Play account, not to an account with us.

Restoring a subscription

On a new phone or after reinstalling the app, open Settings (the gear on the scanner) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

Cancelling a subscription

In the app, open Settings and tap Manage subscription. Or go there directly: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep access until the period you have paid for ends.

Refunds

Apple and Google take the payment, so a refund is asked of them: from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, and from Google through Google Play. As a consumer in the EU you also have a fourteen-day right of withdrawal, which you exercise the same way.

Redeeming a code

If you have been given a code for the subscription, redeem it in the App Store app: tap your profile picture at the top right, then Redeem Gift Card or Code. The subscription then shows up in the app by itself.

Your data

The app has no account and sends us nothing by itself, so there is nothing to delete unless you have emailed us. If you have, write and we will show you what we have or delete it.
