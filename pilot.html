Get started as a pilot user

Thank you for trying The Sweetener Guide. As a pilot user you get the subscription free. Choose your phone and follow the steps.

On iPhone

  1. Redeem your code

    Tap the button. The App Store opens with the code filled in. Tap Redeem and confirm with Face ID or your Apple password.

    Redeem the code in the App Store

  2. Get the app

    When the code is redeemed, the App Store offers to download The Sweetener Guide. If it does not, find the app here:

    The Sweetener Guide on the App Store

  3. Open the app and allow the camera

    The app asks for the camera the first time you open it. It needs the camera to read the label.

  4. Point at the ingredients list

    Hold the phone still over the ingredients list on a product. When the corners in the picture turn green, the app takes a picture by itself. There is nothing to press.

  5. Read the result

    The app shows which sweeteners and sugars it found, and how they affect blood sugar.

If the link does not work

Get the app first. Open it, tap the gear at the top right, choose Redeem code and type this code:

GRATIS26

About the subscription

The code gives you The Sweetener Guide free for one year. After that the subscription continues at the normal price unless you cancel it. You cancel in the app: tap the gear, then Manage subscription. The code only works if you do not already have a subscription.

Requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

On Android

  1. Get the app

    Tap the button and install The Sweetener Guide from Google Play.

    Get it on Google Play

  2. Open the app and allow the camera

    Tap Allow camera. The app needs the camera to read the label.

  3. Redeem your code

    1. Tap the gear at the top right and choose See subscription.
    2. Choose Monthly and tap Start subscription.
    3. In Google Play's window, tap the payment method and choose Redeem code.
    4. Type this code and confirm:

    VELKOMMEN26

    Google asks for a payment method even though the first 90 days are free. Nothing is charged until the 90 days have passed.

  4. Point at the ingredients list

    Hold the phone still over the ingredients list on a product. When the corners in the picture turn green, the app takes a picture by itself. There is nothing to press.

  5. Read the result

    The app shows which sweeteners and sugars it found, and how they affect blood sugar.

About the subscription

The code gives you The Sweetener Guide free for 90 days. After that the subscription continues at 9 kr. a month unless you cancel it. You cancel in the app: tap the gear, then Manage subscription. The code can be used until 31 December 2026, and only if you have not subscribed before.

Requires a phone with Android 8 or later.

Getting a good scan

  • Use good light, and get the whole ingredients list in the picture.
  • If the text is small, zoom with two fingers. Tap the picture to focus.
  • Does a result look wrong? Tap the speech bubble at the top left and send us a message. That is exactly what the pilot is for.
